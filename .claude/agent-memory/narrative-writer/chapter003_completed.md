# Chapter 003: 菜鸟初战 (Completed)

## Core Scene Sequence
1. **School end + banter** (~500 chars): Lin Xiaoxiao teases Mo about sports — sets up normalcy before the break
2. **The chill + warning** (~200 chars): Xiaobai warns of witch via telepathic link 【】
3. **Sending Lin away** (~300 chars): Bad excuse ("forgot something"), Lin looks back once — suspicion seed planted
4. **Barrier entrance** (~400 chars): Sound disappears first → grey spreads from edge → purple-grey sky, distorted edges, rotten sweet smell
5. **Witch reveal** (~250 chars): Humanoid, no face, black mist + dark red vein patterns, glides not walks
6. **Clumsy transformation** (~300 chars): Nervous, forgets steps, Xiaobai snaps "专心" — uses 【】 for telepathic speech
7. **Wild shooting** (~300 chars): Most miss, hits don't stick, witch recovers
8. **Getting wrecked** (~300 chars): Slammed into wall, right sleeve torn + right forearm scraped, staff knocked away, magic depleted
9. **Awakening moment** (~200 chars): Xiaobai: "你哥明天早上叫谁起床" — brother image flashback motivaes her
10. **Brick punch + comeback** (~300 chars): Picks up brick, punches witch (stubbornness), picks up staff, gathers all remaining magic
11. **Final beam** (~150 chars): White light beam destroys witch, barrier dissolves
12. **Aftermath** (~250 chars): Collapses shaking, Xiaobai "平均线以上", she laughs "好刺激"
13. **Walk home** (~100 chars): Stops downstairs, looks at hands
14. **Next day school** (~300 chars): Lin asks casually, Mo lies, Lin says "行" — Mo knows she didn't believe

## Key Design Decisions
- **Xiaobai's role**: Physical blocker removed, replaced by verbal wake-up using brother as emotional lever — ties back to Chapter 001 sibling dynamic
- **Right arm injury**: Happens during wall impact (right shoulder hits wall) — torn sleeve + forearm scrape — aligns with Chapter 004 bandage continuity
- **Lin's suspicion**: Not overt — "行了" and drops it, but Mo knows she didn't believe. Establishes Lin's pattern: observes, doesn't push, doesn't forget
- **Battle style**: Pure magic blasts (seed stage), no shadow abilities yet — stubborn physical fighting (brick punch) shows character
- **Transmission marks**: 【 】 for Xiaobai's telepathic speech — distinguishes from regular dialogue
- **Chapter end**: No summary/sublimation — ends on concrete scene (next day school, Lin's doubtful "行")
